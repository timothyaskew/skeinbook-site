Privacy Policy

SkeinBook · Effective March 9, 2026

Overview

SkeinBook is a desktop application for managing your personal knitting and crochet pattern library. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data SkeinBook collects, how it's used, and your choices.

What Data SkeinBook Collects

Data Stored Locally on Your Computer

All of the following stays on your machine and is never sent to our servers:

  • Pattern metadata — titles, designers, yarn weights, needle sizes, gauge, and other details extracted from your PDFs
  • PDF file paths — SkeinBook tracks where your files are located but does not copy or upload them
  • Thumbnails — generated from the first page of your PDFs, stored locally
  • Settings — your preferences (theme, sort order, API keys)

Data Sent to Third-Party Services (Only When You Opt In)

ServiceWhenWhat's SentWhy
Ravelry API When you connect your Ravelry account Your Ravelry OAuth token (stored locally) To sync your Ravelry library, download patterns you own, and display Ravelry thumbnails
Anthropic (Claude AI) When you click "Analyze" on a pattern Extracted text from your PDF (first ~4,000 characters) To identify pattern metadata (title, designer, yarn weight, etc.)
Supabase metadata cache Automatic for non-Ravelry patterns File hash + extracted metadata (title, designer, etc.) To help other SkeinBook users skip redundant AI analysis for identical PDFs
Sentry On application crashes Crash stack traces, OS info, app version (no personal data) To identify and fix bugs

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information
  • We do not track your usage or browsing behavior
  • We do not collect your PDF files or their contents (only extracted text is sent to Claude when you explicitly request analysis)
  • We do not sell or share any data with advertisers

Ravelry Data — Compliance

SkeinBook complies with the Ravelry API Terms of Service. Specifically:

  • Ravelry data (pattern details, thumbnails, user library info) is stored locally only and is never uploaded to any external server, including the Supabase metadata cache
  • The Supabase cache has a compliance guard that explicitly blocks any record with a Ravelry ID from being submitted
  • All Ravelry API interactions are logged locally for audit purposes
  • Your Ravelry OAuth token is stored only on your local machine

Data Retention

  • All data is stored on your computer. If you uninstall SkeinBook, your data remains at:
    • Windows: %APPDATA%\skeinbook\
    • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/skeinbook/
  • You can delete this folder at any time to remove all SkeinBook data
  • The Supabase metadata cache retains anonymous metadata (file hashes + pattern info) indefinitely to benefit the community

Your Choices

  • Don't connect Ravelry — SkeinBook works fully offline without it
  • Don't add a Claude API key — you can manually edit pattern metadata instead
  • Disable crash reporting — toggle off in Settings (coming soon)
  • Export your data — use the JSON export feature to get a full copy of your library metadata
  • Delete your data — uninstall and delete the app data folder

Children's Privacy

SkeinBook is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as we add features. Changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
