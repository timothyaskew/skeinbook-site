SkeinBook is a free desktop app that brings your entire knitting & crochet pattern collection into one searchable, beautiful library. Import PDFs, sync Ravelry, and let AI handle the rest.

Get the Beta Learn More

Everything you need in one app

📄

Import PDF patterns

Drag and drop or browse to add patterns from your computer. Supports bulk import of hundreds of files at once.

🤖

AI-powered metadata

Automatically identifies title, designer, yarn weight, gauge, needle sizes, and more from your PDFs using Claude AI.

🔗

Ravelry sync

Connect your Ravelry account to sync your purchased patterns. Direct links to pattern pages, designers, and yarn on Ravelry.

🔍

Search & filter

Full-text search across all your patterns. Filter by designer, yarn weight, craft type, needle size, and custom tags.

💾

Backup & export

Export your library as ZIP, CSV, or JSON. Restore backups on any machine. Your data is always yours.

🔒

Privacy-first

All data stays on your computer. No accounts required, no cloud storage, no tracking. Connect to Ravelry only if you want to.
